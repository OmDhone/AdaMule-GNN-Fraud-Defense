# 03. Every Single Feature Explained

AdaMule includes an extensive suite of interactive features. Here is an in-depth guide to all 10 major features:

### 🖼️ Visual Feature Map of the Command Center
![Cyber War Room Feature Map](images/04_cyber_war_room_features.png)

---

### 1. 🚨 Red Team Syndicate Heist Simulator
- **What it is**: A playable criminal console where you play as the syndicate boss.
- **Controls**:
  * **Loot Slider**: Set stolen funds between ₹1,00,000 and ₹50,00,000.
  * **Smurfing Toggle**: Split payments into micro-chunks below the ₹50,000 threshold.
  * **Merchant Camouflage Toggle**: Spray micro-transfers to grocery shops and chai vendors.
  * **Temporal Jitter Toggle**: Add 4-hour delays between hops.
  * **Layering Hops**: Choose 2, 3, or 5 hops deep.
- **Execute Heist**:
  * Triggers procedural sirens and laser pulses.
  * Launches 60+ animated cash particles flowing through the network.
  * Displays a dual AI comparison: **Legacy GCN gets fooled (94% evasion)**, while **AdaMule intercepts the funds before ATM cashout!**

---

### 2. 🛡️ Blue Team Defense Sentinel & Physics Graph
- **What it is**: A high-tech cyber defense monitoring station.
- **Features**:
  * **60 FPS Force-Directed Physics Graph**: You can click and drag accounts around the screen; nodes bounce and repel like physical objects.
  * **Legitimacy Lens Slider (0% to 100%)**:
    - At 0% (Standard GCN): *Sharma Kirana Store* turns **RED** (False Positive Alert).
    - At 100% (AdaMule): *Sharma Kirana Store* turns bright **EMERALD GREEN** (Protected!), while the true criminal ring is highlighted in **RED**.
  * **Countermeasure Buttons**:
    - `⚡ Run Continuous Graph X-Ray`: Scans all nodes and edges.
    - `❄️ Freeze All Flagged Mule Nodes`: Instantly locks all criminal nodes with ice-lock badges.
    - `👁️ Unmask Camouflage Trails`: Strips away fake decoy transactions.

---

### 3. 🌐 3D WebGL Holographic Cyber Globe (Three.js)
- **What it is**: A full 3D interactive model of Earth floating in a starry spacefield.
- **How to use**: Click the **`🌐 3D Cyber Globe`** button in the header.
- **What it shows**:
  * Visualizes cross-border **Hawala & International Laundering Corridors**.
  * Shows funds moving across **Mumbai $\to$ Delhi $\to$ Dubai $\to$ Zurich $\to$ London $\to$ Singapore $\to$ Hong Kong $\to$ New York**.
  * Glowing 3D ballistic Bezier curves arch into space above the Earth's surface.
  * Mouse drag rotates the globe; scroll wheel zooms in and out.

---

### 4. 🎙️ Voice-Activated AI Forensic Copilot (Web Speech API)
- **What it is**: Talk to the system through your microphone like a real military or intelligence operations room.
- **How to use**: Click **`🎙️ Voice AI`** in the header.
- **Spoken Voice Commands**:
  * *"Freeze account"* $\to$ Freezes the selected node; the AI speaks back: *"Account frozen. Precautionary hold applied."*
  * *"Trace ring"* $\to$ Unmasks topological dependencies to the ATM cashout hub.
  * *"Execute heist"* $\to$ Launches the syndicate attack.
  * *"3D globe"* $\to$ Switches to the 3D WebGL cyber globe hands-free!
  * *"Show graph"* $\to$ Switches back to the 2D physics graph.
  * *"Generate SAR"* $\to$ Opens the official legal Suspicious Activity Report.

---

### 5. 🎯 Gamified Cyber Detective Campaign (Story Mode)
- **What it is**: A 3-level interactive detective campaign with progressive difficulty and XP points.
- **Missions**:
  * **Mission 1: The Campus Smurfs (Rookie • +250 XP)**: Catch a 2-hop student mule ring laundering phished tuition fees.
  * **Mission 2: The Kirana Smokescreen (Detective • +500 XP)**: Exonerate the grocery store and isolate the camouflaged structuring ring.
  * **Mission 3: Operation Ghost Mixer (Master • +1000 XP)**: Defeat a 5-hop RL gradient-evasion attack with temporal jitter.
- Earn rank promotions: **Field Agent $\to$ Senior Analyst $\to$ Chief Inspector**.

---

### 6. ⚡ Live Chaos Engine & Stress-Tester
- **What it is**: Inject extreme simulated banking crises in real-time to test model resilience.
- **Chaos Events**:
  * 💥 **SIM-Swap Surge**: 100 simultaneous account takeover events to test velocity rate dampers.
  * 🛍️ **Diwali Festival Rush (10x Load)**: 10x consumer retail surge to prove AdaMule maintains zero false alarms on honest merchants during peak holiday volume.
  * 🎭 **Syndicate Adaptive Retaliation**: Attacker algorithm dynamically recalculates graph paths around frozen nodes.

---

### 7. ⛓️ Crypto & Web3 Peel-Chain / Mixer Forensics
- **What it is**: Detects cryptocurrency money laundering on Ethereum and Tron.
- **Features**:
  * Click **`⛓️ Crypto`** in the header and hit **`🔍 Trace On-Chain Peel Chain`**.
  * Scans mempools to unmask a **$100,000 USDT Peel Chain** peeling funds into sub-$10,000 micro-chunks across 5 hops into Uniswap and privacy pools.
  * Broadcast blacklist flags to blockchain compliance oracles.

---

### 8. 📁 1-Click Court-Admissible Evidence Bundle Exporter
- **What it is**: Generates an official digital evidence bundle for law enforcement (Police, ED, CBI, or FIU-IND).
- **How to use**: Click **`📁 Evidence`** in the header.
- **Output**: Automatically downloads a structured JSON file (`ADAMULE_CASE_EVIDENCE.json`) containing node KYC profiles, edge classifications, model calibration metrics, and FATF typology tags.

---

### 9. 📱 Simulated PhonePe / UPI Smartphone Mockup
- **What it is**: A functional mobile phone simulation embedded inside the war room.
- **Features**:
  * Select payee (Student Mule vs. Sharma Kirana Store).
  * Select amount (₹250, ₹5,000, ₹48,500, ₹90,000).
  * Tap **`Pay via UPI PIN`** $\to$ triggers live radar scan:
    * If paying a mule $\to$ **PAYMENT INTERCEPTED** (red alert & alarm sound).
    * If paying a verified merchant $\to$ **PAYMENT APPROVED** (green checkmark & chime).

---

### 10. 📊 Streamlit Multi-Tab Research Dashboard
- **What it is**: An academic dashboard built in Streamlit for in-depth data exploration.
- **Pages**:
  * *Network Overview*: Global graph density, degree distributions, and scenario proportions.
  * *Ego-Network Explorer*: Zoom in on individual accounts and their 1-hop and 2-hop neighborhoods.
  * *Adversarial Simulator*: Test individual attack budgets and action spaces.
  * *Model Benchmark Comparison*: View ROC-AUC, PR-AUC, and Hard-Negative FPR tables.
